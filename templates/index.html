The Random Forest model yields superior Mean Absolute Error (₹2.89L), capturing non-linear interactions between skills, city hubs, and seniority.

Pipeline Feature Engineering

Text Feature Pipeline (Job Text = Title + Skills) TF-IDF Vectorizer with 500 max features, English stop-words, and 1-2 n-grams.
Categorical Pipeline (Location) Most-frequent imputer with One-Hot Encoding (min_frequency=20 for rare cities).
Numerical Pipeline (Experience & Company Ratings) Median Imputation + StandardScaler on min/max experience, review volume, and ratings.
Log-Target Transformation Target transformed via log1p(y) to manage positive salary skewness; reversed with expm1.
